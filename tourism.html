Bilbao

Bilbao is the largest city in the Basque Autonomous Community and a major economic and cultural center.

Historically an industrial port city, Bilbao underwent extensive urban redevelopment during the late twentieth century. The opening of the Guggenheim Museum Bilbao in 1997 became an international symbol of this transformation.

Visitors can explore:

  • Guggenheim Museum Bilbao
  • Casco Viejo (Old Town)
  • Bilbao Riverfront
  • Mercado de la Ribera

San Sebastián and the Coast

San Sebastián (Donostia) is internationally known for its beaches, architecture, and culinary culture.

The city's most famous beach, La Concha, curves around a sheltered bay and is frequently ranked among Europe's most scenic urban beaches. Beyond San Sebastián, the Basque coastline offers fishing villages, dramatic cliffs, and Atlantic landscapes.

Popular coastal destinations include:

  • La Concha Beach
  • Zurriola Beach
  • Getaria
  • Zumaia Flysch Coast

Dining and Activities

Food is central to the visitor experience in the Basque Country. The region has earned international recognition for both traditional cooking and innovative gastronomy.

Visitors often enjoy:

  • Pintxo bars
  • Seafood cuisine
  • Hiking in the Pyrenees and coastal trails
  • Surfing along the Bay of Biscay
  • Local festivals and cultural events
