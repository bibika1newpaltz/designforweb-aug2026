The Basque Country: Europe’s Distinct Atlantic Culture

The Basque Country is a cultural region located on both sides of the western Pyrenees, spanning northern Spain and southwestern France. Although politically divided between different administrative territories, the region is united by a shared language, Euskal culture, and a strong sense of historical identity.

Anthropologists and historians have long been interested in the Basques because of their unique language, Euskara, which has no proven relationship to any other living language. Alongside this linguistic distinctiveness, the region is known for its maritime traditions, mountain communities, celebrated cuisine, sports, and folklore.
